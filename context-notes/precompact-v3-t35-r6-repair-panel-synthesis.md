## Cleanup-control contract

Only the current destructive reaper lease may reserve, spawn, release, retry, signal, or unlink. Nonholders may request or observe only. Every cleanup `tmux -N` action uses the accepted no-auth gate pattern and a monotone create-only epoch:

`CONTROL_RESERVED -> CONTROL_IDENTIFIED_GATE_CLOSED -> CONTROL_RELEASED_ACTION_POSSIBLE -> CONTROL_RESULT_CAPTURED? -> CONTROL_PRINCIPAL_ABSENT -> CONTROL_RECONCILED`.

There is at most one nonterminal control epoch. The action is selected from an authority-owned exact allowlist of read-only server/session/pane observation, `kill-server`, and final absent-server read-only no-autostart query. No `new-session`, `send-keys`, shell, arbitrary target/format, or auth/canary is permitted. A released action may have occurred even without a receipt; recovery first proves the client/group absent, then re-observes current target state. A possibly spawned but unidentified cleanup client is post-release FAIL/quarantine. “Application clients absent” precedes cleanup controls; “all tmux clients absent” is legal only after the final no-autostart control principal is absent.
